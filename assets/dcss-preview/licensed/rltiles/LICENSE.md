# RLTiles — stairs

`stairs_down.png` and `stairs_up.png` are two tiles cut out of `tile.png` of the
Dungeon Crawl 2D tileset of **RLTiles**, <http://rltiles.sf.net> (archive
`dctile.zip`, downloaded 07.10.2026 from rltiles.sourceforge.net). The only
change is that the sheet's teal background colour was made transparent.

License: public domain. RLTiles is a graphical tileset for roguelike games
that its authors have placed in the public domain. Its authors
ask that the following notice travels with every copy:

> Part of (or All) the graphic tiles used in this program is the public
> domain roguelike tileset "RLTiles".
> Some of the tiles have been modified by DNG Codex.
>
> You can find the original tileset at:
> http://rltiles.sf.net

Contributors named by RLTiles: Denzi, Alex Korol, Edger, Wan-ichi, So-Miya,
Haruko Numata, Tatsuya, AllegroHack tiles (Kelly Youngblood, Paul Pliska, John
Harris), Dainokata, Zmy.

The game recolours these tiles per place (`tools/dcss-rpg-stairs.js`).
