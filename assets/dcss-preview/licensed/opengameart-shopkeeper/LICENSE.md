`keeper.png` (84×84) — the author's portrait: the opaque black around the figure was made transparent (flood fill from
the edges) and, since 2026-10-16, its eight colours are remapped onto the game's palette (muted teal, leather, rust, bone;
ember eyes) by `tools/atlas/derive-shop-scene.py`. The untouched download is kept in `tools/atlas/source/`. Used as the merchant of the outfit counter in
the menu. The shop scene, light, flame and eye layer around him are drawn by the same script
(`derived/ui/shop/`).
